


	Member 2 — ModR/M + SIB + Addressing Modes

		This member handles the ModR/M and SIB machinery.

		This is probably the most technical part.

	1. ModR/M byte

		Explain that ModR/M contains:

			7 6 | 5 4 3 | 2 1 0
			----+-------+-------
		 	MOD |  REG  |  R/M

		So:

			ModR/M = MOD + REG + R/M

			Each section has a specific purpose.

		MOD

		Determines whether the operand is:

			00 → memory, usually no displacement
			01 → memory + 8-bit displacement
			10 → memory + 32-bit displacement
			11 → register

		2. All MOD cases

			Give examples.

			MOD = 00
			MOV EAX, [EBX]
	
			No displacement.

			MOD = 01
			MOV EAX, [EBX+8]

			8-bit displacement.

			MOD = 10
			MOV EAX, [EBX+1000]

			32-bit displacement.

			MOD = 11
			MOV EAX, EBX

			Both operands are registers.


		3. 32-bit registers encoding table

			Register 	Encoding
			
			EAX		000
			ECX		001
			EDX		010
			EBX		011
			ESP		100
			EBP		101
			ESI		110
			EDI		111

		SIB byte

		SIB means:
	
		Scale-Index-Base

		It has:

		7 6 | 5 4 3 | 2 1 0
		----+-------+-------
		SCALE| INDEX | BASE
